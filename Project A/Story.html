Aftermath: Welcome to The Mayhem

Aftermath follows a group of people living in a divided town named Centralpoint. In the past Centralpoint was a unified urban town which promoted a lot of business, but now is suffering from power corruption and violent outbreaks. Those who enter the city are subjected to the trauma which prevents them from leaving because of a permanent barrier that causes mental disarray aka distortion in them. click the story title

Jin Xang: The Fall Of Unity

The Jin Xang series is a 4 part story that was inspired by different shows and books that I can’t recall. The main character is Jin Xang (obviously) who lives in a world/society that has elemental and mythical powers. The powers were created by the era 0 elites (known as the gods and goddesses). Azalea (Life\Nature), Oriyal (Death), Akata (Fire), Crystalline, (Water\Ice), Angellina & Aura (Sanity\Insainty). Element powers included Fire, Water, Ice, and Nature, (Venom, Acid, and Electric come later in the series). Mythical powers are powers that are considered to be relating to the goddess of life (Azalea) and the god\goddess of death (Oriyal) or Angellina & Aura. (More plot coming soon)
